Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

At astrong.xyz, we respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how data is collected, stored, and processed across our primary website and all subdomains.

1. Information We Collect

When you access or use astrong.xyz, certain technical and telemetry data is automatically collected by our universal client scripts:

  • Device & Session Identifiers: An anonymized, unique 8-character uppercase alphanumeric Device ID (e.g., astrong_device_id) and Session ID (astrong_session_id) stored via root cookies (.astrong.xyz) and browser storage.
  • Hardware & Graphics Diagnostics: WebGL GPU vendor and renderer details, screen resolution, viewport dimensions, screen orientation, color scheme preferences (dark/light mode), and reduced motion preference settings.
  • System & Network Specifications: User-Agent string (browser name/version, operating system), connection type (RTT, downlink bandwidth estimate), and battery status (charge level and state).
  • Network & Geolocation Metadata: IP address, approximate geographical region (city, region, country), and ISP/organization name fetched via IP lookup APIs.
  • Usage Telemetry: Aggregated visit counters, first-seen timestamps, session duration, and navigation interactions.

2. How We Use Collected Data

The collected telemetry and information are strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • Monitoring site traffic, unique device counts, and performance metrics across subdomains.
  • Maintaining security, preventing automated abuse, and protecting site infrastructure.
  • Displaying diagnostic debugging information within developer utilities and administration dashboards.
  • Persisting user interface preferences (such as selected accent colors, theme modes, and tool configurations).

3. Analytics & Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party infrastructure providers to support analytics and backend storage:

  • Google Analytics (gtag.js): Used to measure website traffic and aggregate visitor statistics under property tag G-ZY51JBLESQ.
  • Google Firebase Firestore: Device telemetry records and session metadata are written securely to the devices Firestore database collection for analytics and online status monitoring.

We do not sell, rent, or trade telemetry data to advertising networks or third-party data brokers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

astrong.xyz uses browser cookies set on the root domain (.astrong.xyz) to maintain session continuity across subdomains. If cookie permissions are restricted, local storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) is used as a fallback on the main site.

You can clear cookies and browser storage at any time through your web browser settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at austin@astrong.xyz.
